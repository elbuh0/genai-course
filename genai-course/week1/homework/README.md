I built a VAE and two different types of GANs (not counting a deliberately triggered mode-collapse variant). What surprised me was that the Fashion-MNIST VAE I built generated sharper outputs, at least to my eyes, than the MNIST VAE, even though intuitively (and quantitatively, given its higher FID score) its task of generating more visually complex clothing items as opposed to simple numbers should incur a higher reconstruction loss. I was also surprised when navigating my VAE’s latent space, specifically that one dimension appeared to encode such high level information as item type (shoe or sweater, in one run). I thought the dimensions would encode finer-grade details.
